Why Choose a Catamaran Over a Standard Boat?

Many travelers wonder if a boat tour is actually worth the price tag. Traditional single-hull boats tend to rock heavily with the ocean swells, which can easily ruin an afternoon for anyone prone to motion sickness.

Catamarans use a twin-hull design that distributes weight evenly across the water. This wider stance provides significantly more stability during the 60-minute sailing route. Having a licensed captain and engineer onboard ensures a smooth journey along the southern coast of South Korea.

With over 100,000 guests served since 2011 and official partnerships with luxury properties like the Shilla Hotel and Parnas Jeju, the operation relies on strict safety standards. It is one of the few marine activities in the region holding top-tier safety ratings.

The 60-Minute Coastal Route: What You Will See

The journey departs from Daepo Port and hugs the rugged volcanic shoreline of Seogwipo. As part of a UNESCO-recognized geological area, the cliffs here formed thousands of years ago through ancient volcanic activity.

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A natural sea arch bearing a striking resemblance to an elephant drinking from the ocean.
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): Vertical hexagonal rock pillars carved entirely by nature.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon & Wolpyeong Sea Cave: Deep, shadowy chasms accessible only from the water.
  • Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul): The largest sea cave along this stretch of the Jeju coastline.
  • Pangpaengideok: A wide, flat coastal reef plateau perfect for catching the fading afternoon light.

What Is Included: Food, Drinks, and Fishing

One of the main selling points of this tour is the onboard hospitality. Unlike other operators that charge extra for refreshments, the food and drinks are entirely complimentary and available for unlimited refills.

  • Drinks: Fresh Korean draft beer, red and white wine, local Jeju tangerine juice, and bottled water.
  • Snacks & Meals: Jeju local cookies, traditional treats, and hot Korean ramyeon cooked fresh on the boat.
  • Fishing Experience: Rods, reels, and bait are provided free of charge. You can try catching local species like rockfish, scorpionfish, filefish, and puffer fish with a catch-and-release option.

Evaluating the ROI of a travel activity comes down to time versus experience. At around ₩70,000 for the sunset slot, the tour costs less than a standard dinner in Seoul or Jeju City while delivering a completely different perspective of the island.

The autumn golden hour bathes the basalt columns in warm amber light, creating an ideal setting for photography without the harsh summer glare.

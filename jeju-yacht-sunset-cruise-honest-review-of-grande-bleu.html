The 60-Minute Coastal Route

The standard route covers some of the most striking coastal formations in South Korea, many of which are difficult or impossible to see properly from the walking paths on land. During the 60-minute journey, you will cruise past:

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A natural sea cliff bearing an uncanny resemblance to an elephant drinking from the ocean.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon: Deep vertical cliff walls carved into the dark basalt.
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): Hexagonal volcanic rock pillars formed by rapidly cooling lava, sharing geological traits with UNESCO-listed sites.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave and Large Sea Cave: Mysterious sea caverns where the waves echo dramatically against the stone.
  • Pangpaengideok: A broad, flat coastal reef platform that catches the autumn sunlight.

As the afternoon light softens into an autumn sunset, the 360-degree panoramic views over the water provide an ideal backdrop for photography.

Onboard Fishing Experience

Midway through the cruise, the crew sets up a casual fishing session from the stern of the catamaran. All rods, tackle, and bait are provided free of charge. The waters around Seogwipo are home to local species like rockfish (우럭), scorpionfish (쏨뱅이), filefish (쥐치), and pufferfish (복어). It is a straightforward, beginner-friendly activity that adds a bit of hands-on entertainment to the cruise, with a catch-and-release option for anyone who prefers not to keep their catch.

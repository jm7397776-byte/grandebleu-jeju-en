Your Private Party at Sea: Unpacking the Premium Details

A 60-minute cruise might sound short, but it's perfectly curated to maximize your enjoyment without overstaying its welcome. The route showcases some of Seogwipo’s most dramatic coastal geology, including natural wonders that are best appreciated from the water.

Your Jeju yacht tour will take you past the remarkable Wolpyeong Basalt Columns, a UNESCO-style geological wonder of hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs, and the striking Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi). You'll also encounter the Jingotnae Sea Canyon, Wolpyeong Sea Cave, and the expansive Pangpaengideok reef. These are panoramic views you simply can't get from land.

But the experience isn't just about the scenery. It's also a floating feast. Forget overpriced snacks; Grande Bleu offers a generous array of free unlimited refreshments onboard.

What's on offer?

  • Crisp Korean fresh brew draft beer
  • A selection of red and white wines
  • Authentic Jeju tangerine juice, fresh-squeezed
  • Bottled water
  • Delicious Jeju local snacks, including cookies and traditional treats
  • And for a truly unique touch, freshly cooked Korean ramyeon (instant noodles) prepared right onboard!

It’s an all-inclusive culinary journey designed to complement the stunning backdrop. This no-extra-charge policy means you can truly relax and indulge without worrying about your budget – a significant ROI for any luxury traveler.

A Touch of Adventure: Fishing and Dolphin Spotting

For those seeking a little interaction, a beginner-friendly fishing experience is included in your Jeju catamaran adventure. All equipment, including rods and bait, is provided free of charge. You might reel in local Jeju species like rockfish, scorpionfish, filefish, or puffer fish. It’s a fun, low-pressure activity with a catch-and-release option, adding a touch of playful adventure to your romantic cruise.

Keep an eye out for wild Indo-Pacific bottlenose dolphins in their natural habitat. While sightings are never guaranteed with wildlife, sailing through their known migration paths adds an element of thrilling anticipation. It's these small, authentic moments that truly enrich your Jeju travel story.

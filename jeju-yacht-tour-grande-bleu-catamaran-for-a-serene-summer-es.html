The Coastal Highlights Course (60 Minutes)

Your sailing journey from Daepo Port will glide past several natural wonders, offering prime photo ops and a sense of discovery:

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A natural cliff formation eerily resembling an elephant.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon: A deep cliff canyon with dramatic vertical walls, a geological marvel.
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): Hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs, part of Jeju's UNESCO Global Geopark. These geological formations are truly captivating.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave: A mysterious sea cave where waves crash, creating an atmospheric soundscape.
  • Pangpaengideok: A broad, sea-level rock platform, perfect for appreciating the island's unique geology.
  • Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul): The biggest sea cave along this stretch of coast.

If you opt for the sunset cruise, these formations are bathed in 360-degree panoramic golden hour views, a truly unforgettable sight. It's an ideal setting for capturing incredible photographs or simply enjoying the tranquil beauty.

Fishing, Food, and Unwinding on the Water

One of the standout features of the Grande Bleu experience is the complimentary fishing opportunity, included on all tours. Even if you've never held a fishing rod, the crew provides all equipment and bait, making it completely beginner-friendly. You might just catch local Jeju species like rockfish, scorpionfish, filefish, or even a puffer. Catch-and-release is an option, allowing you to enjoy the thrill without keeping the fish. It’s a wonderfully calming activity, letting you connect with the ocean.

And yes, food and drinks are covered too! Grande Bleu offers unlimited, complimentary refreshments onboard:

  • Korean ramyeon: Instant noodles, cooked fresh onboard – a surprisingly comforting treat on the sea.

This attention to detail, from the provided fishing gear to the free unlimited refills, highlights Grande Bleu's commitment to a seamless, enjoyable experience. It means you can fully relax and immerse yourself in the journey without worrying about extra costs.

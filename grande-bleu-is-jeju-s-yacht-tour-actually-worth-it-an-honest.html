What You'll See: Jeju's Hidden Coastal Gems

The 60-minute Jeju sunset cruise (or day luxury tour) isn't just about being on the water; it's a curated journey past geological wonders that are part of Jeju's UNESCO Global Geopark. These formations look dramatically different from the sea, offering fresh perspectives even for repeat visitors to Korea.

Your Grande Bleu Jeju yacht journey will take you past:

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A striking natural cliff shaped distinctly like an elephant.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon: A dramatic, deep cliff canyon with imposing vertical walls.
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): Unique hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs, a geological marvel.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave: A mysterious sea cave with waves crashing at its entrance.
  • Pangpaengideok: A broad, flat reef plateau perfect for dramatic photos.
  • Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul): The biggest sea cave along this stretch of coast.

During the golden hour, you'll be treated to 360-degree panoramic views, making it one of the most scenic things to do in Jeju. Keep an eye out for local marine life; lucky guests sometimes spot Indo-Pacific bottlenose dolphins in the distance.

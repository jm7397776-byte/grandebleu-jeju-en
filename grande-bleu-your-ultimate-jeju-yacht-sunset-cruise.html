Unveiling Jeju's Coastline: A Perspective Beyond the Road

You've explored Jeju by car, perhaps even by tour bus. But what about the view from the sea? This is what nobody tells you about Jeju travel: some of the most dramatic landscapes are best appreciated from the water. Grande Bleu is not just a Jeju yacht; it's Korea's only certified catamaran brand, meaning superior stability and comfort for your journey. With two robust vessels, each 17.1m in length and weighing 40 tons, the experience is smooth, spacious, and undeniably premium. Our licensed captain and engineer onboard ensure your safety, allowing you to simply relax and soak it all in.

The 60-minute Jeju sunset cruise offers a carefully curated route that showcases Jeju's geological wonders. You'll glide past the imposing Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi), a natural cliff carved by the sea. Then, the boat navigates into the Jingotnae Sea Canyon, with its dramatic vertical walls, a sight often compared to the majestic fjords of Norway, yet distinctly Jeju. This Jeju sailing experience provides an "Instagrammable but not touristy" perspective on these natural marvels, a true highlight for any Jeju itinerary.

Beyond the Scenery: Fishing and Unique Coastal Gems

The Grande Bleu Jeju yacht tour isn't solely about admiring the views; it's an interactive Jeju activity. Every cruise includes a casual fishing experience. Fishing rods and bait are provided free of charge, and our crew is happy to assist beginners. You might catch real Jeju species like rockfish (우럭), scorpionfish (쏨뱅이 (쏨뱅이), filefish (쥐치), or even the occasional puffer (복어). It's a fun, relaxed way to connect with the island's marine life, with a catch-and-release option available.

The coastal route continues to impress, taking you to the UNESCO-style Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli), magnificent hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs that attest to Jeju’s unique geological history. You’ll also explore the mysterious Wolpyeong Sea Cave with its crashing waves, the broad Pangpaengideok (wide flat reef plateau) – a truly photogenic spot, and the impressive Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul), the biggest on this stretch of coast. The entire route is designed to culminate in a 360-degree panoramic golden hour view, a spectacular sight available once a day during our Jeju sunset cruise.

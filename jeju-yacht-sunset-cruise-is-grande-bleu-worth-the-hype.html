Autumn Sunsets on the Seogwipo Coast

The highlight of any Jeju sailing adventure in autumn is undoubtedly the Jeju autumn sunset. As the day draws to a close, the sky transforms into a canvas of gold and crimson, painting a mesmerizing spectacle over the horizon. The 60-minute Grande Bleu Sunset Tour departs around 5:20 PM, perfectly timed to catch this daily wonder.

From the deck of the catamaran, you'll witness a 360-degree panoramic display of the golden hour, providing unmatched opportunities for photography. The cruise takes you past iconic geological wonders of the Seogwipo Jeju coastline, including:

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A distinctive natural cliff formation resembling a majestic elephant.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon: Deep, dramatic cliff canyons with imposing vertical walls.
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): Impressive hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs, reminiscent of UNESCO World Heritage sites, showcasing Jeju's unique geology.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave: A mysterious sea cave where waves crash, creating a natural symphony.
  • Pangpaengideok: A wide, flat reef plateau offering unique photographic perspectives.
  • Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul): The largest sea cave along this stunning stretch of coast.

If luck is on your side, you might even spot wild Indo-Pacific bottlenose dolphins swimming in their natural habitat along the Seogwipo coast. While sightings aren't guaranteed, they are frequent and add an unforgettable touch to your Jeju travel experience.

What to Expect on Your Grande Bleu Jeju Yacht Adventure

A typical sunset cruise Jeju departs from Seogwipo’s Daepo Port, timed perfectly to catch the day's most breathtaking light. As the sun begins its descent, the sky transforms into a canvas of deep oranges, pinks, and purples. This 60-minute journey takes you past remarkable geological formations along the Jeju Korea coastline.

You'll glide past the iconic Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi), the dramatic Jingotnae Sea Canyon, and the hexagonal columns of Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli) – a stunning example of volcanic geology. Further along, you might glimpse the mysterious Wolpyeong Sea Cave and the broad Pangpaengideok reef plateau, perfect for panoramic photos.

And here’s what nobody tells you: Grande Bleu ensures you won't go hungry or thirsty. They offer free unlimited refills on Korean draft beer, red and white wine, fresh-squeezed Jeju tangerine juice, and bottled water. Feeling peckish? Enjoy complimentary Jeju local snacks and even freshly cooked Korean ramyeon onboard – a surprisingly satisfying treat at sea.

Beyond the Views: Fishing and Friendly Faces

The experience on a Grande Bleu Jeju catamaran goes beyond just sightseeing. Included in your tour is a casual fishing experience. Rods and bait are provided free of charge, and it's beginner-friendly. You might even catch local Jeju fish like rockfish (우럭) or scorpionfish (쏨뱅이)! It’s a fun, low-pressure activity that adds a touch of local flavor to your Jeju activity. Catch-and-release is available, making it enjoyable for everyone.

The seasoned crew, backed by over 100,000 guests served since 2011 and partnerships with 5-star hotels like Shilla Hotel and Parnas Jeju, ensures a smooth and enjoyable trip. They're prepared for anything: rain ponchos are provided if it drizzles, blankets if it gets chilly in Jeju October, and even motion sickness bags for those who might need them. This thoughtful service elevates the experience, particularly for first-time visitors to Korea.

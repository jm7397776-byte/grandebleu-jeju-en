The Grande Bleu Experience: Unpack the Value

A 60-minute journey around Seogwipo's stunning coastline offers more than just pretty views. This isn't just a quick boat ride; it’s an all-inclusive floating lounge experience. The course takes you past geological wonders that tell tales of Jeju's volcanic past.

You'll glide past:

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A natural cliff shaped strikingly like an elephant.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon: Deep, dramatic cliff canyon with vertical walls.
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Jusangjeolli): Hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs, part of Jeju's UNESCO Global Geopark. These are truly spectacular from the water.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave: A mysterious sea cave where waves crash dramatically.
  • Pangpaengideok: A wide, flat reef plateau, perfect for photos.
  • Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul): The biggest sea cave along this stretch of coast.

All of these are best appreciated from the unique perspective of the ocean. Imagine capturing a 360-degree panoramic view during the golden hour – an incredible sunset cruise Jeju memory.

Hook a Local Catch: Free Fishing Fun

For a touch of adventure, all tours include a fishing experience. Don't worry about bringing gear; rods and bait are provided free of charge. You might reel in local Jeju species like rockfish (우럭), scorpionfish (쏨뱅이), filefish (쥐치), or even a puffer (복어).

It’s beginner-friendly, and a catch-and-release option is available. It adds another layer of fun to your Jeju activity, making it more engaging than just passive sightseeing.

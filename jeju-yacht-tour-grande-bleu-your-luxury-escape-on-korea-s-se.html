Unveiling Jeju's Coastline: A Unique Perspective

The 60-minute route of the Grande Bleu yacht tour is carefully curated to showcase the most dramatic and beautiful sections of Jeju's southern coast. From the calm waters off Daepo Port, you'll glide past a series of natural wonders that are often only seen from a distance or from crowded viewpoints on land.

Witness the majestic Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi), a natural cliff formation that truly resembles an elephant's profile, standing sentinel over the sea. Marvel at the intricate patterns of the Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli), hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs reminiscent of a UNESCO site, formed by ancient lava flows cooling against the ocean. You'll also encounter the mysterious Wolpyeong Sea Cave and the dramatic vertical walls of the Jingotnae Sea Canyon. Keep your camera ready as you pass Pangpaengideok, a broad sea-level rock platform perfect for capturing unique coastal shots, and the Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul).

The highlight for many, especially during the later excursions, is the opportunity for 360-degree panoramic golden hour views. As the sun dips towards the horizon, painting the sky in fiery hues, the entire coastline transforms into a breathtaking spectacle. And for a touch of unexpected magic, keep an eye out for the wild Indo-Pacific bottlenose dolphins that sometimes frequent these waters – a truly unforgettable bonus to your Jeju sunset cruise.

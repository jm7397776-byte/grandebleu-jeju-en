Unforgettable Views: The Grande Bleu Coastal Cruise Route

Embark on a spectacular 60-minute journey along the stunning Seogwipo coastline. The Grande Bleu Jeju catamaran follows a meticulously planned route designed to showcase Jeju Island's most dramatic natural wonders. This isn't just a cruise; it's a geological tour by sea, revealing what nobody tells you about Jeju's hidden beauty.

You’ll glide past the towering Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi), a natural cliff that truly resembles its namesake. Witness the impressive Jingotnae Sea Canyon with its deep, dramatic vertical walls, and the magnificent Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli), hexagonal volcanic formations recognized as a UNESCO Global Geopark site. Explore the mysterious Wolpyeong Sea Cave and the expansive Pangpaengideok, a photogenic wide flat reef plateau perfect for capturing those postcard-perfect moments. You'll also encounter the Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul), the biggest sea cave on this coast. The highlight for many is the 360-degree panoramic golden hour views during our sunset cruise, an ethereal spectacle you can only experience once a day. Keep an eye out – you might even spot wild Southern bottlenose dolphins!

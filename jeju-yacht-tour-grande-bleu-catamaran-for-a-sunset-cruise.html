Coastal Gems & Unexpected Wildlife Encounters

Beyond the breathtaking sunset, the Jeju sailing route offers a unique perspective on Jeju's dramatic coastline. You'll glide past natural wonders like the magnificent Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi), a natural cliff shaped eerily like an elephant. The journey continues along the striking Jingotnae Sea Canyon and the awe-inspiring Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli), hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs reminiscent of UNESCO World Heritage sites.

Keep an eye out for the mysterious Wolpyeong Sea Cave and the expansive Pangpaengideok reef plateau, offering fantastic photo opportunities. Sometimes, if you're lucky, you might even spot the Indo-Pacific bottlenose dolphins that call these waters home. While sightings aren't guaranteed (they are wild animals, after all), encountering these majestic creatures freely swimming in their natural habitat adds an unforgettable layer of magic to your Jeju yacht adventure.

The Route Less Traveled: Seogwipo's Coastal Wonders by Sea

Our 60-minute journey departs from Daepo Port in Seogwipo, offering a perspective of Jeju's dynamic coastline you simply can't get from land. As the catamaran glides smoothly, powered by both engine and sail, you'll witness breathtaking geological formations and dramatic sea scapes. This is where the photo opportunities truly come alive, offering an "Instagrammable but not touristy" backdrop.

The course includes a series of iconic landmarks along the coast:

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A natural cliff sculpted by the elements to resemble an elephant's head and trunk.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon: A deep, dramatic cliff canyon with impressive vertical walls.
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): Part of Jeju's UNESCO Global Geopark, these hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs are a testament to the island's fiery past, similar to the Giant's Causeway in Ireland.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave: A mysterious sea cave, where waves crash inside, creating a captivating spectacle.
  • Pangpaengideok: A broad, sea-level rock platform, perfect for dramatic coastal photography.
  • Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul): The biggest sea cave on this stretch of coast.

We also often pass through sections known as wild southern bottlenose dolphin habitats. While sightings aren't guaranteed, the chance to spot these magnificent creatures in their natural environment adds an extra layer of magic to your Jeju sailing adventure. For those on the sunset tour, these sights are bathed in the warm glow of the setting sun, offering 360-degree panoramic golden hour views – a truly unforgettable spectacle that happens only once a day.

More Than Just Sailing: Fishing, Romance, and Corporate Bonding

A Jeju catamaran trip isn’t solely about sightseeing; it’s an interactive experience. Included in all tours is a beginner-friendly fishing activity. Even if you've never held a rod, our crew provides all the equipment and bait you'll need, completely free of charge. You don't need to bring a thing.

You'll have the chance to catch real local Jeju fish species, including:

  • Rockfish (우럭)
  • Scorpionfish (쏨뱅이)
  • Filefish (쥐치)
  • Puffer (복어)

It’s a fun, engaging activity for all ages, and we encourage a catch-and-release approach, allowing you to enjoy the thrill without harming the marine ecosystem. For couples, it’s a playful way to bond. For corporate teams, it's a fantastic Jeju team building exercise, fostering camaraderie in a relaxed setting. Especially during the winter months, the enclosed cabin offers a cozy refuge, making it a perfect Jeju winter warm trip activity.

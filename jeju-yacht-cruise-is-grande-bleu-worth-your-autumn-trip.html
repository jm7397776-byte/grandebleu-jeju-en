Unveiling Jeju's Untouched Coastline: The 60-Minute Sailing Route

The real magic of the Jeju sunset cruise is the journey itself. Departing from Daepo Port in Seogwipo, Jeju, the Grande Bleu takes you along a meticulously planned 60-minute route designed to showcase the island's most stunning and often unseen geological wonders. This course is definitely Instagrammable, but in a way that feels natural and awe-inspiring, not overly touristy.

You'll glide past:

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A natural cliff shaped uncannily like an elephant – a truly photogenic spot.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon: A deep, dramatic cliff canyon with towering vertical walls.
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): These hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs are reminiscent of UNESCO Global Geopark sites, a testament to Jeju's incredible geological history.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave & Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul): Mysterious sea caves where waves crash and echo.
  • Pangpaengideok: A broad, flat reef plateau perfect for appreciating the vastness of the ocean.

As the day ends, the true spectacle unfolds: the 360-degree panoramic golden hour views. This once-a-day event paints the sky in fiery oranges, purples, and reds, reflecting off the calm Seogwipo waters. It’s a moment of pure, unadulterated beauty that feels a world away from your corporate duties.

Adding to the fun, a casual fishing experience is included on all tours. Fishing rods and bait are provided free, making it incredibly beginner-friendly. You might just reel in a rockfish (우럭), scorpionfish (쏨뱅이), filefish (쥐치), or even a puffer (복어)! Catch-and-release is an option, making it a low-impact, high-thrill activity. Many guests "highly recommend trying it!"

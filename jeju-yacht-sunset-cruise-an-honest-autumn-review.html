The Reality of Seogwipo's Autumn Coastline

October brings crisp air, clear skies, and calm coastal waters to southern Jeju Korea. Back on shore, viewing spots like the Wolpyeong hexagonal basalt columns often feel congested. From the deck of a wide twin-hull catamaran, however, the volcanic architecture takes on genuine scale.

Because catamarans use two parallel hulls rather than a deep single monohull, the lateral roll is drastically reduced. If you normally get seasick on smaller excursion ferries, the stability here is immediately apparent. You move freely between the outdoor teak deck and the heated interior cabin without bracing against every wave.

What the 60-Minute Route Actually Covers

The cruise departs Daepo Port and navigates the dramatic seaward boundary of the UNESCO Global Geopark network along Seogwipo. Unlike large commercial passenger ferries that keep hundreds of metres offshore, this route tracks closer to natural sea-level landmarks:

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A freestanding basalt headland naturally sculpted into an elephant trunk dipping into the surf.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon: Vertical volcanic sea crevices cut by millennia of tides.
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): Striking geometric volcanic formations rising straight out of the ocean.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave & Pangpaengideok: A sheltered sea cavern paired with a wide, flat reef plateau where swell breaks cleanly.
  • Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul): The most cavernous opening along the southern cliff face.

Midway through the journey, the crew cuts the engines on a calm lee shore for a quick hands-on fishing stop. Rods, line, and bait are set up for you. Guests regularly haul in local species like scorpionfish, filefish, and rockfish before returning them to the sea.

Practical Tips for Autumn Sailing

Autumn light fades quickly in South Korea. The golden hour departure leaves promptly at 17:00, catching the amber sunset against Mount Hallasan.

Bring a light windproof jacket or wool layer. While the afternoon sunshine feels pleasant on the sheltered aft deck, offshore evening breezes cool down rapidly the moment the sun dips below the horizon.

Exploring the Wolpyeong Coastline and Volcanic Caves

The 60-minute sailing route focuses on the rugged western coast of Seogwipo, showcasing geological formations designated under the UNESCO Global Geopark network (as recognized by the Jeju Tourism Organization).

During the cruise, the vessel glides past several notable geological highlights:

  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): Towering hexagonal stone pillars formed by ancient cooling lava.
  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A natural stone cliff resembling an elephant drinking from the ocean.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon: A deep, narrow coastal gorge with vertical rock faces.
  • Pangpaengideok: A wide, flat volcanic reef plateau perfect for wide-angle landscape photography.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave & Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul): Deep, mysterious coastal cavities carved out by centuries of wave erosion.

The late afternoon light casts a warm, orange glow across these dark basalt cliffs, creating a spectacular backdrop for photography without the harsh shadows of midday. If you are lucky, you might even spot members of the local Indo-Pacific bottlenose dolphin population that frequent these clean waters.

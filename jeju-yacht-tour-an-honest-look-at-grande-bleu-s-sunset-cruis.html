Unforgettable Coastal Wonders on Your 60-Minute Cruise

The 60-minute sunset cruise Jeju itinerary is carefully crafted to showcase the dramatic coastline of Seogwipo. Departing from Daepo Port, you’ll glide past natural wonders that are best appreciated from the water, offering unique photo opportunities that are both Instagrammable and genuinely awe-inspiring.

Highlights of the Grande Bleu coastal course include:

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A distinctive natural cliff formation resembling an elephant's head and trunk, sculpted by centuries of crashing waves.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon: A deep, dramatic cliff canyon with vertical walls plunging into the sea, revealing Jeju's ancient volcanic geology.
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): These hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs are a geological marvel, part of a UNESCO Global Geopark site, testament to Jeju's fiery past.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave: A mysterious sea cave where waves playfully crash, adding a touch of adventure to your cruise.
  • Pangpaengideok: A broad, photogenic sea-level rock platform, perfect for capturing the vastness of the ocean.
  • Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul): The biggest sea cave along this coastline, a truly impressive natural landmark.

As you sail, you'll get a 360-degree panoramic view, especially stunning during the golden hour. The changing light paints the sky and sea in hues of gold, orange, and purple, creating an ever-shifting masterpiece. Keep an eye out for wild dolphins – this area is a known habitat for the majestic Indo-Pacific bottlenose dolphin, offering a rare treat if you're lucky!

Unveiling Jeju's Coastal Secrets: The Grande Bleu Route

The 60-minute tour departs from Daepo Port in Seogwipo, taking you along one of Jeju’s most picturesque coastlines. What truly sets this experience apart is the close-up view of natural wonders, some of which are part of Jeju's UNESCO Global Geopark.

From the deck of the yacht, you'll encounter a series of geological marvels:

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A natural cliff strikingly shaped like an elephant.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon: A deep, dramatic cliff canyon with impressive vertical walls carved by nature.
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): These hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs are a geological wonder, presenting a unique "organ pipe" formation.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave: A mysterious cave where waves crash and echo.
  • Pangpaengideok: A wide, flat reef plateau, perfect for scenic views.
  • Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul): The biggest sea cave along this stunning coast.

Seeing these formations from the sea offers a fresh perspective that's truly "what nobody tells you" about Jeju. It's a different world from the land-based tours, making it an ideal choice for repeat visitors. The sunset tour, available once daily, promises 360-degree panoramic golden hour views that are genuinely unforgettable.

Cast a Line, Catch a Memory: Onboard Fishing & Refreshments

What makes the Grande Bleu experience truly stand out is the inclusion of activities that add a playful touch to your romantic voyage. Fishing is a popular activity, and even if you've never held a rod before, the friendly crew makes it simple and enjoyable.

You don't need to bring anything; fishing rods and bait are provided absolutely free. Local species like rockfish (우럭), scorpionfish (쏨뱅이), filefish (쥐치), and even puffer fish (복어) are commonly caught. It's a beginner-friendly experience, and many guests are genuinely surprised by their success! While typically catch-and-release to protect the local ecosystem, it's the thrill of the "hand-맛" (the feel of a fish on the line) that creates lasting memories.

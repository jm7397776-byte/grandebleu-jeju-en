The 60-Minute Coastal Route

The journey departs from Seogwipo and heads along the southern coastline. Because this stretch of Korea is part of a protected marine zone, the views remain pristine. Licensed captains and engineers navigate past several notable landmarks during the 1-hour route:

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A natural sea cliff shaped remarkably like an elephant drinking from the ocean.
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): Hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs formed by cooling lava, echoing the geological wonders found at UNESCO-listed sites.
  • Pangpaengideok: A broad, flat coastal rock plateau that catches the autumn sunlight.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave and Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul): Mysterious basalt caves accessible only by water.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon: Deep, vertical cliff formations carved by centuries of ocean tides.

Onboard Activities: Fishing and Unlimited Refreshments

Beyond the sightseeing, a Jeju sailing trip on this twin-hull vessel includes hands-on activities. Crew members hand out complimentary fishing rods and bait, allowing guests to try catching local species such as rockfish (우럭), scorpionfish (쏨뱅이), filefish (쥐치), and puffer (복어). Everything caught is released back into the sea to preserve the local marine ecosystem.

While out on the deck, the floating food bar remains fully stocked with free unlimited refills. Pour yourself a glass of white wine or grab a hot bowl of Korean ramyeon while watching the autumn sunset paint the Seogwipo sky in shades of amber and violet.

Why a Catamaran Actually Matters (Not Just Marketing)

Most Jeju boat tours run single-hull vessels. Fine on a calm day, less fine when your 70-year-old mother-in-law is turning green over the railing.

A catamaran sits on two hulls, which cuts the roll dramatically. Grande Bleu is the only Jeju operator with the top-tier safety certification that lets guests board without wearing life vests the whole time — a small detail that says a lot about how the boat handles.

For families travelling with older parents or younger kids, that stability is the whole reason to pick this over a cheaper option. We've had guests admit they took motion sickness pills for nothing.

The 60-Minute Coastal Course

Daepo Port sits on Seogwipo's south coast, right next to the famous Jusangjeolli cliffs. The route hits sights you genuinely cannot see from land:

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi) — a cliff formation shaped, unsurprisingly, like an elephant
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns — hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs, part of Jeju's UNESCO Global Geopark geology
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave — the yacht noses in close as the waves crash
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon — deep vertical cliff walls
  • Pangpaengideok — a wide flat reef plateau that photographs beautifully at golden hour
  • Keunbada Donggul — the largest sea cave on this stretch of coast

The land-side viewpoint at Jusangjeolli gets around 2 million visitors a year. Approaching the same columns from the water, with nobody's selfie stick in your frame, is a completely different experience.

Jeju in October: Why Autumn Is the Sweet Spot

Summer crowds thin out, the humidity drops, and the light softens. October and November deliver the Jeju autumn sunset that photographers actually plan trips around — long golden hour, clear horizons, and sea temperatures still mild enough for a comfortable deck.

The sunset cruise departure shifts with the season. In autumn, boarding typically lands between 5:00 and 6:00 pm, so double-check when you book. Luxury (daytime) tours run up to five times daily starting 11 am.

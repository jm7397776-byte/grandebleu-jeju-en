Jeju's Hidden Coastal Gems: A 60-Minute Scenic Course

As the Grande Bleu smoothly glides from Daepo Port, prepare for a sight that’s exclusive to those on the water. You'll witness a spectacle of geological wonders that are either obscured or entirely invisible from land:

  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): These magnificent hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs, reminiscent of UNESCO World Heritage sites, rise dramatically from the sea, forming a natural屏풍 (folding screen) around you.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave: Nestled amongst the columns, this mysterious sea cave reveals itself, waves crashing rhythmically at its entrance.
  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A remarkable natural cliff formation that truly resembles an elephant's trunk dipping into the ocean, a sight you can only appreciate fully from the sea.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon: A deep cliff canyon with dramatic vertical walls, showcasing Jeju's raw volcanic power.
  • Pangpaengideok: A broad, sea-level rock platform, perfect for capturing those unique, Instagram-worthy shots without the typical tourist throngs.
  • Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul): The biggest sea cave along this stretch of coast, offering an imposing natural arch.

The 60-minute route is carefully curated to showcase these natural sculptures, culminating in 360-degree panoramic golden hour views if you opt for the sunset tour. In winter, the air is often clearer, allowing for stunning, vibrant sunsets that paint the sky in fiery hues, making for truly unforgettable photo moments.

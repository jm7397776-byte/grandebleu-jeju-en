Why a Catamaran Makes Sense for Solo Travelers

If you are traveling alone in South Korea, comfort and stability matter. Traditional single-hull boats tend to rock heavily with the waves, which can ruin a peaceful afternoon or trigger motion sickness.

Grande Bleu utilizes a twin-hull design that disperses water pressure, making the ride remarkably stable. You do not have to worry about clinging to the railing or feeling dizzy. This stability creates an environment where you can comfortably read a book, sip a drink, or just stare at the horizon without distraction.

Furthermore, the atmosphere on board is calm and unpretentious. With a maximum capacity of 91 passengers on a spacious deck, you never feel crammed into a corner. It is easy to find a quiet bench near the bow to watch the waves pass by.

The 60-Minute Coastal Route: What You Will See

The boat departs regularly from Daepo Port in Seogwipo, heading out along some of the most rugged coastline in South Korea. The captain steers the vessel close to volcanic geological formations that are difficult to appreciate properly from the mainland.

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A natural sea cliff shaped remarkably like an elephant drinking from the ocean.
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): UNESCO-style hexagonal rock pillars formed by cooling lava centuries ago.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave & Large Sea Cave: Deep geological cavities carved out by crashing waves over generations.
  • Pangpaengideok: A broad, flat coastal shelf that glows in the afternoon light.

The autumn breeze is crisp, making the outdoor deck comfortable as long as you wear a light jacket. As the sun begins to drop, the golden hour light reflects off the basalt cliffs, creating an ideal setting for photography enthusiasts.

Try Your Hand at Deck Fishing

If you want a more interactive experience, the crew provides fishing rods and bait completely free of charge. You do not need any prior experience.

The waters around Seogwipo are home to local species such as rockfish (우럭), scorpionfish (쏨뱅이), filefish (쥐치), and pufferfish (복어). It is a casual, catch-and-release activity that adds a bit of fun to the journey. If you do not catch anything, the unlimited snacks more than make up for it.

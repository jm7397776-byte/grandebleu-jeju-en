Experience the Underrated Beauty of Seogwipo's Coastline

The Grande Bleu sails from Daepo Port in Seogwipo, venturing along a breathtaking 60-minute coastal route that showcases Jeju's unique geology. This isn't just open-sea sailing; it’s a guided tour past some of the island's most iconic natural formations.

You'll glide past the majestic Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi), a natural cliff that truly resembles its namesake. Further along, the Jingotnae Sea Canyon reveals dramatic vertical walls, a geological marvel. A highlight for many are the Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli), hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs reminiscent of Giant's Causeway, an impressive UNESCO-style geological wonder. The tour also includes views of the mysterious Wolpyeong Sea Cave, the wide flat Pangpaengideok reef plateau, and the largest Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul) on the coast. These aren't just scenic spots; they're geological treasures that offer incredible photo opportunities, making your trip truly Instagrammable without feeling overly touristy.

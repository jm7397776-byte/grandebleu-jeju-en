Coastal Route: UNESCO Geopark Formations from the Water

The 60-minute coastal route traces geological wonders along the Seogwipo coastline that remain largely hidden from land-based observation points. As the catamaran glides past Daepo Port, passengers encounter towering volcanic topography formed thousands of years ago.

Key coastal milestones include:

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A basalt sea stack sculpted by wind and waves resembling an elephant trunk dipping into the ocean.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon: A narrow, cliff-flanked saltwater inlet showcasing dramatic vertical stone drop-offs.
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): Ancient hexagonal volcanic pillars protected within the UNESCO Global Geopark network.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave and the Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul): Expansive maritime grottoes carved into the base of southern cliffs.
  • Pangpaengideok: A flat volcanic reef plateau offering exceptional panoramic ocean backdrops.

During golden hour, the yacht provides uninterrupted 360-degree horizons as the autumn sky shifts from amber to deep violet. Lucky travelers may also spot local Indo-Pacific bottlenose dolphins surfing the catamaran's wake.

Hands-On Sea Fishing for Beginners and Children

Midway through the route, the crew anchors temporarily near quiet coastal shallows to initiate an approachable sea fishing experience. All lightweight rods, pre-rigged tackle, and bait are supplied free of charge.

Under the guidance of the crew, children and parents easily drop lines to target indigenous reef species. Common catches include rockfish (우럭), scorpionfish (쏨뱅이), filefish (쥐치), and pufferfish (복어).

The onboard fishing focuses on accessible fun and gentle catch-and-release handling rather than competitive sport. Wide safety railings, non-slip surfaces, and available life vests ensure that children remain safe while experiencing the thrill of their first catch.

For international visitors comparing options for Jeju with kids yacht outings, this hands-on encounter delivers memorable engagement without requiring specialized angling skills.

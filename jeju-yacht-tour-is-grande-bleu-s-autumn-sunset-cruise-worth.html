Uncovering Jeju's Hidden Coastal Gems from the Sea

Departing from Daepo Port in Seogwipo, the Grande Bleu catamaran takes you along a breathtaking stretch of coastline, revealing geological wonders often missed from land. This 60-minute route is meticulously planned to showcase some of Jeju Island's most impressive natural formations:

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A distinct natural cliff shaped remarkably like an elephant, an iconic photo spot.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon: Deep, dramatic cliff canyons with vertical walls plunging into the sea, offering truly impressive vistas.
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): These hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs are a UNESCO-style geological marvel, strikingly clearer and more dramatic from the ocean than from the coastal walking paths.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave: A mysterious sea cave where waves crash, creating a serene, almost meditative atmosphere.
  • Pangpaengideok: A broad, flat reef plateau, perfect for capturing those golden hour moments.
  • Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul): The biggest sea cave along this stunning coast.

For an October anniversary cruise, the autumn light enhances these features, painting the basalt columns and sea caves in warm hues. You’ll experience 360-degree panoramic golden hour views – a spectacle available only once a day. This journey truly provides a fresh perspective on Jeju's unique volcanic landscape.

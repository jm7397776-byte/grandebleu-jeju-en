Coastal Wonders: The Grande Bleu Jeju Itinerary

Your Jeju sailing journey embarks from Daepo Port in Seogwipo, tracing a magnificent 60-minute route along the southern coast of Jeju Island, Korea. The scenery unfolding before you is nothing short of impressive, showcasing Jeju's unique volcanic geology.

Highlights of the Jeju sunset cruise route include:

  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): These spectacular hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs, part of a UNESCO Global Geopark, rise dramatically from the ocean.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave & Large Sea Cave: Mysterious sea caves where waves crash and echo, adding a touch of drama.
  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A natural cliff shaped remarkably like an elephant, a popular spot for photos.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon: A deep, dramatic cliff canyon with striking vertical walls.
  • Pangpaengideok: A broad, flat reef plateau that's incredibly photogenic, especially as the light softens.

You'll enjoy 360-degree panoramic views throughout, particularly during the golden hour on the sunset cruise. This blend of natural artistry makes it Instagrammable, yet distinctively not overrun by typical tourist crowds.

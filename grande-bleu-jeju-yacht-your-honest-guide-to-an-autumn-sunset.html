The Scenic Route: Daepo Coastline Highlights

The 60-minute Jeju sailing route is carefully curated to showcase the stunning geological wonders of the Seogwipo coast. You’ll glide past:

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A dramatic natural cliff shaped distinctly like an elephant's trunk, a fantastic photo opportunity.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon: Deep, vertical cliff walls carving into the sea, showcasing Jeju’s volcanic power.
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): These hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs, reminiscent of UNESCO Geopark sites, are a truly unique sight.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave: A mysterious sea cave where waves crash dramatically.
  • Pangpaengideok: A broad, flat reef plateau, perfect for absorbing the vastness of the ocean.
  • Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul): The biggest sea cave along this stretch of coast.

As the catamaran navigates these iconic landmarks, your professional crew – licensed captains and engineers – ensure a smooth journey. Their expertise means you can simply relax and enjoy the 360-degree panoramic golden hour views.

Fishing for Fun: A Unique Jeju Activity

Adding another layer of fun to your Jeju travel experience is the complimentary fishing opportunity. Fishing rods and bait are provided free of charge, making it super easy for beginners. You might even catch some genuine Jeju local species like rockfish (우럭), scorpionfish (쏨뱅이), filefish (쥐치), or even a puffer (복어)! The option for catch-and-release means it's all about the experience, not necessarily dinner.

This brief, beginner-friendly fishing session is a great way to interact with the ocean and create some playful memories, especially if you’re traveling with friends or on a relaxed honeymoon.

Dolphins in Jeju? A Serendipitous Encounter

One of the most exciting, albeit unpredictable, highlights of the Grande Bleu Jeju cruise is the chance to spot wild dolphins. While we can't guarantee an encounter (they are, after all, wild animals), the route often passes through areas frequented by bottlenose dolphins. Imagine the thrill of seeing a dorsal fin glide past the yacht, silencing conversations and drawing everyone's gaze to the water.

When luck is on your side and dolphins appear close by, the crew will naturally help you capture those magical moments with photos. It's these unplanned, natural occurrences that truly elevate the experience beyond a standard boat trip.

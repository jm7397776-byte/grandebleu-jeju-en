The Grande Bleu Experience: A Relaxing Journey Along Seogwipo's Coast

Your journey begins at Daepo Port in Seogwipo, Jeju, a much less bustling spot than the main city areas. This makes for an easy arrival and departure, allowing you to truly unwind from the moment you step aboard. Grande Bleu operates two impressive twin-hull luxury sailing yachts, the 44-seater 550ho and the 47-seater 620ho, ensuring a comfortable and stable ride.

Once underway, the yacht glides smoothly along the coast, revealing sights that are largely inaccessible by land. The 60-minute course is carefully curated to showcase Jeju Island's geological marvels, transformed by the late afternoon sun into a golden panorama.

Here’s what you’ll witness:

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A striking natural cliff shaped unmistakably like an elephant's head, glowing golden in the sunlight.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon: Deep, dramatic cliff canyons with imposing vertical walls, showcasing Jeju’s volcanic power.
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): Part of a UNESCO Global Geopark site, these incredible hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs are a testament to ancient lava flows meeting the ocean.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave: A mysterious sea cave where waves crash rhythmically against ancient rock.
  • Pangpaengideok: A wide, flat reef plateau that's incredibly photogenic, especially during the golden hour.
  • Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul): The biggest sea cave along this stretch of coast.

The entire journey offers 360-degree panoramic golden hour views, a truly unforgettable spectacle that happens just once a day.

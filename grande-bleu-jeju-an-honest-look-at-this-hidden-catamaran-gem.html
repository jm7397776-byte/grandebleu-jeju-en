Your 60-Minute Coastal Cruise: What You'll Actually See

The 60-minute Grande Bleu Jeju yacht tour departs from Daepo Port (GPS coordinates: 33.2287°N 126.4318°E) in Seogwipo, taking you along the stunning southern coast of Jeju Island. This is where you'll witness geological wonders and unique rock formations that tell the island's volcanic story.

Your route includes spectacular sights like:

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A natural cliff shaped uncannily like an elephant.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon: Deep cliff canyons with dramatic vertical walls carved by the sea.
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): Hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs, reminiscent of a UNESCO Geopark site.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave: A mysterious sea cave where waves crash dramatically.
  • Pangpaengideok: A broad, sea-level rock platform, perfect for dramatic photos.
  • Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul): The biggest sea cave along this coastline.

If luck is on your side, you might even spot wild Indo-Pacific bottlenose dolphins swimming alongside the yacht. While not guaranteed (they are wild, after all!), it's an exhilarating bonus. The highlight for many, especially during a spring evening, is the 360-degree panoramic golden hour views offered only once a day on our Sunset Tour.

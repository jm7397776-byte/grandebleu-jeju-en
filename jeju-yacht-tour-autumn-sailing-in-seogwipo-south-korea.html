Exploring Soesokkak and Seogwipo in Autumn

Autumn in Jeju brings clear skies and an understated stillness to the island's coastal geography. A classic day in Seogwipo often begins where fresh alpine water from Mount Halla meets the ocean at Soesokkak Estuary.

Walking along the pine-shaded boardwalks of Soesokkak during early morning or late afternoon lets you admire emerald channels set against dark basalt formations. It is a peaceful land-based introduction to Jeju before heading to the open sea.

To view the coastline from a fresh perspective, private charter and small-group voyages depart daily from nearby Daepo Port. You can discover more background details on our About Grande Bleu page.

Coastal Highlights: UNESCO Geology and Hidden Caves

During the 60-minute sailing route, the catamaran navigates close to Seogwipo’s most dramatic volcanic geology. Guests aboard the autumn golden hour voyage enjoy front-row views of coastal landmarks:

  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Jusangjeolli): Hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs formed by cooling basaltic lava, showcasing Jeju's UNESCO Geopark heritage.
  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A natural sea stack sculptured by waves to resemble an elephant dipping its trunk into the ocean.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon: Deep vertical sea cliffs framing narrow turquoise inlets.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave & Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul): Mysterious sea caverns carved into ancient rock faces.
  • Pangpaengideok: A broad basalt plateau stretching out at sea level.

For non-English speaking international visitors seeking search information, this iconic route is recognized as 济州游艇, 韩国游艇, 济州夕阳, 济州航海, 韩国济州岛 in Chinese, and 済州ヨット, 韓国ヨット, 済州サンセット, 済州旅行, 韓国済州島 in Japanese.

The onboard selection includes:

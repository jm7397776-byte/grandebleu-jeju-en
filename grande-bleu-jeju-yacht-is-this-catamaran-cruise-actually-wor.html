Coastal Wonders: Your 60-Minute Grande Bleu Itinerary

Departing from Daepo Port in Seogwipo, Jeju, the catamaran smoothly navigates a scenic 60-minute route designed to showcase Jeju's unique geological heritage. GPS coordinates: 33.2287°N 126.4318°E.

You'll get a prime view of several coastal highlights, which are part of Jeju's UNESCO Global Geopark:

  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): Stunning hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs, reminiscent of the Giant's Causeway or other UNESCO sites.
  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A natural cliff shaped strikingly like an elephant's trunk, a fantastic photo spot.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon: A deep cliff canyon with dramatic vertical walls carved by the sea.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave: A mysterious cave at sea level where waves crash rhythmically.
  • Pangpaengideok: A broad, flat reef plateau, offering another unique perspective.
  • Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul): The biggest sea cave along this stretch of coast.

The stability of the twin-hull catamaran (licenced captain & engineer onboard) means you can relax and fully immerse yourself in these natural wonders, making it a perfect Jeju activity for all ages.

What to Expect on the 60-Minute Sailing Route

Departing from Daepo Port in Seogwipo, the catamaran moves smoothly across the water thanks to its twin-hull design. This stability makes it a comfortable choice for travelers who might usually worry about seasickness on standard single-hull boats.

The route hugs the southern coastline of Jeju, passing through several notable geological formations recognized for their volcanic history:

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A natural sea cliff shaped like an elephant dipping its trunk into the ocean.
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): Dramatic hexagonal volcanic pillars formed by rapidly cooling lava.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave & Large Sea Cave: Mysterious basalt caverns carved out by decades of heavy wave action.
  • Pangpaengideok: A wide, flat rock plateau near the water's level that offers a striking contrast to the sheer cliffs.

Licensed captains and engineers manage the navigation, allowing you to focus entirely on the scenery or photography. On clear days, the late afternoon golden hour lights up the basalt columns with a deep orange hue.

Food, Drinks, and Onboard Experience

One detail that surprises many international visitors is the open-amenities policy during the cruise. Everything served at the onboard refreshment bar is included in your ticket price with no hidden fees or token systems.

What to Expect on the 60-Minute Coastal Route

The journey departs from Daepo Port in Seogwipo Jeju and navigates a protected southern marine route. You will view dramatic geological formations shaped by ancient volcanic activity along the Jeju Korea coastline. The route covers several notable landmarks:

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A natural cliff formation resembling an elephant drinking from the sea.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon: Deep, vertical cliff walls dropping sharply into deep blue water.
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): Hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs formed by rapid cooling lava.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave and Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul): Mysterious sea caves echoing with ocean swells.
  • Pangpaengideok: A broad, flat coastal reef plateau perfect for wide-angle horizon views.

Unlike standard speedboats, the twin-hull design of a catamaran minimizes pitching and rolling. This makes the journey remarkably stable for guests in their 60s and beyond, ensuring a smooth ride even when coastal winds pick up during autumn afternoons.

Interactive Onboard Fishing Experience

Every sailing includes a brief, optional fishing session suitable for all experience levels. The professional crew provides all necessary rods, lines, and bait completely free of charge.

You might catch local Jeju marine species such as rockfish (우럭), scorpionfish (쏨뱅이), filefish (쥐치), or pufferfish (복어). Participation is entirely optional, and guests can choose to release their catch safely back into the ocean immediately after unhooking. It provides a brief, engaging activity that breaks up the scenic cruising time.

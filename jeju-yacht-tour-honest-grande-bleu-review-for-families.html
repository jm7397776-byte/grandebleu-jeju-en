The 60-Minute Coastal Route: What You Actually See

The sailing course departs Daepo Port in Seogwipo and navigates the southwestern shoreline. You will cruise past Wolpyeong Basalt Columns, a hexagonal formation connected to the broader UNESCO Global Geopark network, without fighting the land-based crowds at the main tourist boardwalks.

The boat sails past Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi), a striking sea cliff resembling an elephant drinking from the ocean, followed by the deep vertical walls of Jingotnae Sea Canyon. Further down, you pass Wolpyeong Sea Cave, the expansive flat plateau of Pangpaengideok, and the Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul).

Cruising in autumn gives you dramatic sea-cliff textures under crisp daylight. While wild Indo-Pacific bottlenose dolphins occasionally surface nearby, sightings depend entirely on nature.

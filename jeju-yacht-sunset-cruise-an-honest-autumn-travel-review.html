Why Take a Sailing Tour on Jeju Island in Autumn?

Autumn in Jeju brings crisp ocean air, clear sky conditions, and comfortable temperatures that make outdoor activities far more pleasant than humid months. The swaying pampas grass along the coast frames the backdrop as you head out from Daepo Port.

While many visitors view the island's famous geological formations from overcrowded cliffside walkways, seeing them from the water offers an entirely different perspective. Instead of jostling for position behind railing fences, you view the volcanic coastline directly at eye level.

For solo travelers or couples seeking a memorable outing without paying private charter prices, sharing a broad twin-hull vessel offers plenty of personal space. The twin hulls provide exceptional stability on the water, making sea-sickness rare even for sensitive passengers.

What the 60-Minute Coastal Route Covers

Departing from Seogwipo Daepo Port, the licensed captain and engineer steer the yacht along a scenic 60-minute loop. The course highlights six distinct natural landmarks along the UNESCO-listed volcanic coast:

1. Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A massive natural basalt cliff naturally eroded into the distinct shape of an elephant drinking from the ocean. 2. Jingotnae Sea Canyon: A narrow volcanic canyon with dramatic vertical basalt walls rising sharply above the tide. 3. Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): Hexagonal geometric cliff structures formed thousands of years ago by cooling lava flows. 4. Wolpyeong Sea Cave: A shadowed cave entrance carved into the cliff face where waves crash against rock walls. 5. Pangpaengideok: A flat, expansive volcanic reef plateau sitting right at sea level. 6. Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul): The most prominent sea cavern opening along this stretches of Seogwipo coast.

Viewing Mount Hallasan towering in the background behind these dark basalt cliffs during golden hour provides fantastic photography opportunities.

Catch-and-release is standard, and beginners receive hands-on instruction from the crew.

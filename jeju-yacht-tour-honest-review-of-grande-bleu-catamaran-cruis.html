Understanding the Seogwipo Coastal Route

A standard Jeju yacht tour departs from Daepo Port in Seogwipo and heads along the dramatic southern coastline. Unlike standard motorboats, a catamaran offers a stable, smooth ride due to its twin-hull design.

The 60-minute journey is carefully mapped out to showcase geological wonders formed millions of years ago. You will cruise past Wolpyeong Basalt Columns, hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs similar to UNESCO-style geological sites, and dramatic formations like Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi).

The route also stops near Wolpyeong Sea Cave and Pangpaengideok, a wide flat reef plateau that creates stunning reflections during golden hour. Having licensed captains and engineers onboard ensures safety while navigating these rugged coastlines.

Choosing Between Daytime and Sunset Tours

Timing your Jeju sailing experience depends entirely on the atmosphere you want.

The daytime tour is ideal for families or travelers who want maximum visibility. The bright midday light makes geological formations like Jingotnae Sea Canyon and the large sea caves exceptionally clear in photographs.

The sunset cruise, departing in the late afternoon, is suited for solo travelers, couples, or anyone seeking a slower pace. As the sun dips toward the horizon, the golden hour light illuminates the southern cliffs of Jeju Island, providing a tranquil environment on the deck.

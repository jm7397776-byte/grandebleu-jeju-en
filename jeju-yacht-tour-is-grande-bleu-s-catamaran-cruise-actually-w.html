Your 60-Minute Jeju Catamaran Cruise: What to Expect Onboard

Every Jeju yacht tour with Grande Bleu is a meticulously crafted 60-minute experience, whether you choose our daytime Luxury Tour or the enchanting Jeju sunset cruise. Our licensed captain and engineer are always onboard, ensuring your safety and a smooth journey.

During your cruise, you'll witness an array of natural wonders along the stunning coastal route. Keep an eye out for:

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A natural cliff dramatically shaped like an elephant.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon: A deep cliff canyon with breathtaking vertical walls.
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): UNESCO-style hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs, a true geological marvel. This area is part of the Jeju Volcanic Island and Lava Tubes UNESCO World Heritage site, adding an extra layer of wonder to your trip.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave: A mysterious sea cave where waves crash dramatically.
  • Pangpaengideok: A broad, sea-level rock platform, perfect for photos.
  • Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul): The biggest sea cave along this stretch of coast.

Complimentary Fishing & Wild Dolphin Spotting

A highlight for many, especially families, is the included fishing experience. We provide all the fishing rods and bait absolutely free. Our friendly crew will assist beginners, making it a fun and accessible activity for everyone – even if you've never held a fishing rod before! You might even reel in some real Jeju fish like rockfish (우럭), scorpionfish (쏨뱅이), filefish (쥐치), or puffer (복어). We encourage a catch-and-release option to protect the local marine ecosystem.

As you glide through the serene waters, especially along the migratory paths near Wolpyeong Basalt Columns, there's a chance to spot wild bottlenose dolphins! These majestic creatures often appear, adding an unforgettable moment to your Jeju sailing adventure. It’s a truly magical sight for kids and adults alike.

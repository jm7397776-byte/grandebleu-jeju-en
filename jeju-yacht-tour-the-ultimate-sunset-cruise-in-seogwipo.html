Why Choose a Catamaran Over Traditional Boats?

When exploring things to do in Jeju, stability on the water matters, especially for couples and business travelers looking to unwind. Traditional single-hull boats tend to rock significantly with the ocean swells.

Catamarans use two parallel hulls connected by a wide frame, which dramatically reduces rolling motion. This design creates a stable, smooth platform for photography, casual conversation, and enjoying a glass of wine without spilling a drop. With a licensed captain and engineer onboard at all times, safety standards remain exceptionally high.

The 60-Minute Coastal Route Highlights

Departing from Daepo Port in Seogwipo Jeju, the vessel navigates a protected coastal route featuring dramatic volcanic formations. Because the area is part of the UNESCO-style coastal geology, the scenery rivals major marine parks in Southeast Asia.

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A natural sea cliff shaped precisely like an elephant drinking from the ocean.
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): Vertical hexagonal rock pillars formed by cooling lava centuries ago.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave & Large Sea Cave: Mysterious marine grottos carved out by relentless wave action.
  • Pangpaengideok: A broad, flat coastal reef plateau that offers striking contrast against the deep blue water.

Onboard Fishing Experience

For those who want a touch of hands-on interaction, the crew provides complimentary fishing rods and bait during a designated stop. You have a solid chance of catching local Jeju marine species such as rockfish (우럭), scorpionfish (쏨뱅이), filefish (쥐치), and pufferfish (복어).

It is a relaxed, family-friendly activity that adds an extra layer of entertainment to the journey. Catch-and-release options are always available for travelers who prefer to admire the fish before letting them swim away.

The 60-Minute Seogwipo Coastal Route

Departing from Daepo Port in Seogwipo, the 60-minute sailing route showcases some of the most dramatic geology in South Korea. The area forms part of the region's volcanic coastline, featuring sheer cliffs and marine erosion caves shaped over millennia.

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A natural sea arch bearing a striking resemblance to an elephant dipping its trunk into the ocean.
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): Hexagonal volcanic rock pillars resembling giant stone pipes carved by cooling lava.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave and Jingotnae Canyon: Deep vertical sea canyons where crashing waves echo against dark basalt walls.
  • Pangpaengideok: A broad, flat coastal reef platform popular for wide-angle horizon shots.

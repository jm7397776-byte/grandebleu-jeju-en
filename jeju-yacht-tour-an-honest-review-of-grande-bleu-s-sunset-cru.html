The Sunset Cruise Jeju Actually Deserves

Imagine leaving the busy mainland behind, sailing into the open waters off Daepo Port in Seogwipo, Jeju Island. The colours of the sky and sea instantly transform. This 60-minute journey isn't just a boat ride; it’s a panoramic experience of Jeju Korea's unique coastline.

During your Jeju autumn sunset cruise, you'll glide past some of Jeju's most breathtaking coastal highlights:

  • Elephant Rock (Kkokkiribawi): A natural cliff dramatically shaped like an elephant's head.
  • Jingotnae Sea Canyon: A deep cliff canyon with awe-inspiring vertical walls.
  • Wolpyeong Basalt Columns (Wolpyeong Jusangjeolli): Unique hexagonal volcanic sea cliffs, reminiscent of a UNESCO geological site.
  • Wolpyeong Sea Cave: A mysterious sea cave where waves crash and echo.
  • Pangpaengideok: A wide, flat reef plateau, perfect for capturing the vastness of the sea.
  • Large Sea Cave (Keunbada Donggul): The biggest sea cave along this stretch of coast.

Catching these geological wonders from the sea offers a perspective you simply can't get from land, especially during the golden hour. The 360-degree views as the sun dips below the horizon are truly something to write home about, providing unparalleled photo moments.
